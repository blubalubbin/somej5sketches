somej5sketches

A small catalogue of p5.js sketches.

  • Rotating sphere
    Adds a coordinate-centre offset and applies the complex-power warp before renormalisation, unlocking a wider family of colour distortions.
  • Fluid simulation
    Interactive Navier-Stokes fluid solver with 32 k particle tracers. Move the mouse to push the fluid.
  • Accretion
    Raymarched accretion disk with fractal sine-noise and cosine colour palette. Based on XorDev's ShaderToy.
  • Creation
    Rippling, glowing grid of colour from a famously compact shader. Based on Silexars' "Creation" on ShaderToy.
  • Starship
    Flashing, trailing debris particles tone-mapped over a sky gradient. Based on XorDev's "Starship" on ShaderToy.
